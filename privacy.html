Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Hermes Backup is an internal tool used only by its owners. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. That lets it see and manage only the files it created itself in the owners' own Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in that Drive.

How the data is used and stored

It uploads encrypted backup archives of the owners' own data to the owners' own Google Drive. It also lists, downloads and deletes those same archives so that old backups can be rotated out and restored. The backups are encrypted before they leave our machine.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising or analytics, and we do not use it to train AI models. Our use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
